Connection Net/Global Label Names:


Power labels:

	VBAT: Battery Voltage (nominal 10.8V)
	3v3: 3.3V voltage
	2v5: 2.5V voltage
	GND: Ground
	VBUS: USB voltage (around 5V, low 4.5V to max 5.25V)


USB-C port (transmit and receive in reference to IMX8X):

	X_USB3_SS3_TX_N: USB-C Transmit Negative Differential Pin; Connected to IMX8X pin B14
	X_USB3_SS3_RX_N: USB-C Receive Negative Differential Pin; Connected to IMX8X pin B17
	X_USB3_SS3_TX_P: USB-C Transmit Positive Differential Pin; Connected to IMX8X pin B13
	X_USB3_SS3_RX_P: USB-C Receive Positive Differential Pin; Connected to IMX8X pin B16

USB-A port (transmit and receive in reference to IMX8X):

	X_USB_OTG2_DN: USB-A Negative Differential Pin; Connected to IMX8X pin B4
	X_USB_OTG2_DP: USB-A Positive Differential Pin; Connected to IMX8X pin B5
